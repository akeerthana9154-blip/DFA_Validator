ABOUT

About Ashoka Women's Engineering College

Autonomous institution, Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh

Our Story

Founded in 2008, the college sits on a 10.6-acre residential campus exclusively for women, offering B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and MCA programs. It is one of the largest colleges in the district.

Location

Located on NH 7 opposite Dupadu Railway Station, Kurnool — well connected by road, rail, and the Kurnool domestic airport.

Accreditations

Recognised by UGC, NAAC, and NBA. Ranked 5th among engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh by IIRF in 2026.

Awards & Recognition

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Award for Best Women's Engineering College in AP (2025), ATAL Achievement Award for Placements (2022), and recognitions from Zee Business (2021) and India Today (2018).

Safety

In-campus hostel with 24/7 CCTV monitoring, ex-army security personnel, and women hostel wardens.
